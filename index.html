    Pre-attack reference 2026–2030 Standards of Cover

    The fifteen SOC operational areas

    The SOC divides the Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone into fifteen operational areas, each with its own Pre-Attack Plan. The SOC does not list the fifteen areas by name — fifteen areas, see SOC printed p. 78 / PDF p. 88. Each Pre-Attack Plan carries, in the SOC's words: a response guide, potential resource needs, fire behavior predictions, lookout locations, specific hazards, heli-spots, hydrant locations, evacuation areas, engine safety zones, staging areas, public refuge areas, medical plan information, evacuation routes, and specific limitations for each area — plus local resource contacts for firefighting resources, city departments, utility agencies, and public assistance providers.

    Not stated in the SOC: which of the fifteen areas cover Trousdale, and the "potential resource needs" listed for each.

    Red Flag posture checklist
    • City Red Flag triggers: humidity 15% or lower, sustained wind 25 mph or more with gusts to 35, burn index 151 or more (City Red Flag parking page).
    • One extra 4-person engine staffed to cover north of Sunset as the "first strike" unit (SOC printed p. 78 / PDF p. 88).
    • Parking restricted on the designated narrow streets in the zone — 16 street segments including Carla Ridge, Loma Vista, N. Hillcrest, Doheny, Coldwater, and Benedict Canyon (City Red Flag page). City Red Flag parking page
    • Weather watch: the Beverly Hills RAWS plus LA County Fire, LAFD, NWS, and South Zone data (SOC printed p. 78 / PDF p. 88).
    • Planning model guideline: on a forecast Santa Ana event, consider pre-positioning 2–3 strike teams in Trousdale and Coldwater rather than one engine — the 2021 Wildfire Assessment Report modeled a Mulholland fire reaching Trousdale in under 15 minutes.
    • Evacuation: Loma Vista, Hillcrest and Doheny are the ways out; traffic control points at Sunset; Genasys and WEA alerts. City evacuation route map (2026)
    • Automatic aid on the first alarm: LAFD BRUSH assignment and LA County Engine 7 (see the Trousdale fixed facts).

    Trousdale fixed facts 2026–2030 Standards of Cover

    • Highest-risk area: Trousdale Estates (Carla Ridge / Loma Vista Dr / N. Hillcrest Rd, up to Mulholland). Second: upper Coldwater Canyon / Loma Linda corridor. SOC Maps 20–23, printed pp. 47–50 / PDF pp. 57–60.
    • What the SOC says a Very High Risk wildland fire needs on the first alarm: 27 personnel (1 BC, 5 engines, 1 truck) = BHFD's whole on-duty force, so any serious Trousdale fire is a mutual-aid fire from the first alarm. SOC critical tasking table, printed p. 117 / PDF p. 127; daily staffing printed pp. 70–72 / PDF pp. 80–82.
    • Planning estimate (wind-driven Santa Ana, first burning period): ~40–50 engines, 2–4 hand crews, 3–4 aircraft, 2–4 water tenders, ~250–320 fire personnel, plus police for evacuation. This is a TRIAGE AND PATROL force, not a one-engine-per-structure defense force (FIRESCOPE doctrine). Planning estimate built on the SOC's staffing and tasking figures; not an SOC or department figure.
    • Speed of the fire: the 2021 Wildfire Assessment modeled a Mulholland ignition reaching Trousdale in under 15 minutes, so pre-positioning, not response, is the only plan that works. 2021 Wildfire Assessment Report (Shew / Lopez).
    • Mutual and automatic aid lineup: LAFD BRUSH assignment (6 engines, 1 light force, 2 helicopters, 2 BCs, 1 assistant chief) — SOC printed p. 71 / PDF p. 81. LA County Fire (E7 first alarm; LF8 + BC second alarm) — printed p. 103 / PDF p. 113. Santa Monica Plan (Sept 2024) with LAFD, LA County, MRCA, NPS — Franklin Canyon = Initial Action Zone — printed pp. 36, 72 / PDF pp. 46, 82.
    • Red Flag day posture: one extra 4-person engine staffed north of Sunset; parking restricted on designated narrow streets. SOC printed p. 78 / PDF p. 88.
    • At stake: ~1,628 parcels, ~$1.6B assessed value; 2,110 public "trees of concern" north of Sunset. SOC printed pp. 35, 41 / PDF pp. 45, 51; trees printed p. 46 / PDF p. 56.
    • The SOC's own admission: "increased response time to the Trousdale Area" is listed as an area requiring improvement; the fixes it offers are a fourth station, a peak-time move-up unit, a two-person quick-response vehicle, or relocating Station 2. SOC printed p. 158 / PDF p. 168 .

    Page references give both numbers: printed page / PDF page (PDF page = printed page + 10). Sources: the 2026–2030 Community Risk Assessment / Standards of Cover and the 2021 Wildfire Assessment Report, both published by the City of Beverly Hills.
